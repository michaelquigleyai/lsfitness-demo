From a garden in lockdown to a members’ gym

Luke started training people from his Braintree home in 2020. In July 2025 he opened LS Fitness HQ at Lakes Farm Business Park: triple the size of his previous sites, renovated over three months, and painted and decorated by Luke himself.

LSF is changing lives one step at a time, taking people from zero gym experience to training 3-4 times a week with purpose.
